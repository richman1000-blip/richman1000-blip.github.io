Privacy Policy

Everyday Unpacked · Updated October 21, 2026

Purpose

Everyday Unpacked is a personal tool used by its owner to prepare and publish educational YouTube videos. It does not offer public account registration.

Data accessed

With the owner's Google authorization, the application reads the connected YouTube channel's identifier, name and upload information. It uploads video files, titles, descriptions and publication settings.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored on the owner's server to maintain the authorized connection. The application does not request Google passwords, Gmail messages or Google contacts.

How data is used

Channel information is used to verify the destination channel, check scheduled uploads and prevent duplicate uploads. Tokens are used only to make authorized requests to Google and YouTube.

Sharing and external services

The application does not sell personal data or use Google user data for advertising or AI model training. Google account data and OAuth tokens are not sent to the AI generation provider.

Video scripts and voice-generation text are sent to DigitalOcean inference services to generate content. Videos and their metadata are sent to YouTube when the owner authorizes an upload.

Storage and security

Credentials are kept in files with restricted access on the owner's server. Channel identifiers, upload records and content files remain stored until the owner removes them. Tokens remain stored until the owner disconnects the application or deletes them.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; stored tokens and local records must also be deleted from the server. Videos already uploaded remain on YouTube until separately deleted there.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no application analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement.
