Everyday Unpacked

A personal video publishing tool for original English educational videos explaining how everyday things work.

What the application does

The application helps its owner prepare video content, upload videos and metadata, and schedule publication on the owner's authorized YouTube channel.

Google and YouTube access

Google authorization is used to identify the connected YouTube channel and upload videos with the owner's permission. This tool is currently for the owner's personal use.
